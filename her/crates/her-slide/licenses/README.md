her-slide draws and edits PowerPoint decks with BetterOffice (Apache-2.0), used from our fork
github.com/Eliseowzy/betteroffice. Its LICENSE and NOTICE are kept here as they ship. The engine
is built without any of its font packs: every face, Latin and CJK, is read from the machine at
run time (Office's own fonts when installed, then macOS's) and none is distributed.
