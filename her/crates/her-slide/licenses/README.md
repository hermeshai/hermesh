her-slide draws and edits PowerPoint decks with NimbleOffice (Apache-2.0), our fork of BetterOffice,
used from github.com/Eliseowzy/nimbleoffice. Its LICENSE and NOTICE are kept here as they ship. The engine
is built without any of its font packs: every face, Latin and CJK, is read from the machine at
run time (Office's own fonts when installed, then macOS's) and none is distributed.
